## Reconcile Before Continuing — premise-change impact review

Human-adopted on 2026-09-23; authority and rationale: [#297 comment5792890835](https://github.com/auguspp/decision-kernel/issues/297#issuecomment-5792890835). This is a standing collaboration principle alongside Reuse First, not a new runtime or approval system.

**Manage what no longer holds, not only what to do next.** When a material premise changes, review its impact and reconcile affected plans before further committing to the affected path. Triggers include changed product acceptance, research policy, architecture boundaries, priorities, demonstrated source/tool capabilities, permission assumptions, or a real failure that would require an unapproved permanent stage or recovery path. A proposed idea or suspected invalidation is not an established fact, adopted policy, implementation or activation.

Restore the current approved #297 direction, exact main and relevant Issue/PR/execution records; pin read-model/work versions separately when state claims require them. Check direct dependencies and evidenced downstream effects across roadmap, active/queued PRs, overlapping or superseded requirements, method/entry instructions, execution permissions, delivery/readers and CI/history obligations. State the inspected scope, unaffected areas and UNKNOWNs. Do not require a whole-repository audit for ordinary edits or already-approved fixes.

**“Continue” preserves the most recent explicit, still-valid objective, scope, budget and stop conditions.** When approval covers an ordered multi-item scope, proceed within it without asking again for each PR, file or routine technical choice. It does not authorize a new permanent stage/state owner, material scope or acceptance change, extra external access or spend, weakened source/identity/CI rules, or a paused/superseded path. Pause only affected new commitments when authority or premises are unresolved; reconcile in-flight calls/writes before any recovery. This principle itself neither cancels running work nor blocks unrelated authorized work.

Use a short receipt in the original Issue/PR/#297: **old premise -> new evidence/instruction -> affected objects and consumers -> disposition -> authority and next step**. Reuse KEEP / CONSOLIDATE / RETIRE / DEFER. For a material rule, method or authority change, name affected runtime/readers, instructions, plans and tests; reconcile them in the same bounded change or state the staged adoption gap. Updating a principle alone does not update its consumers or prove a global cutover.

Requirements Management owns product scope, priority and acceptance reconciliation. Main Construction supplies actual dependency, implementation/run-state and maintenance-cost evidence and implements approved changes. The interaction layer translates this into a clear Human choice: user benefit, simpler/do-nothing alternatives, lasting seam/test/recovery cost, and whether the next step remains within approval. For an approved change, reconcile the current entry summary and affected Issue/PR status or successor links under existing write authority and concurrency checks; do not leave an obsolete title/top-level NEXT pointing at retired work while only appending a buried comment. Preserve deferred/blocked requirements and do not equate Open with execution permission or Closed with implementation success. Report unresolved state/permission conflicts rather than batch-closing, merging or activating by inference.

Every new formal boundary must justify its handoff, recovery, compatibility and test cost. Prefer reuse or fewer boundaries where semantics permit. File counts, adapter counts, test counts and CI duration are investigation signals, not automatic architectural verdicts or approval thresholds. Review must be proportional to actual impact: no unrelated redesign/retest, no speculative framework, and no automatic relaxation of the current complete CI contract.

Obsolete plans do not make historical facts obsolete. Preserve source/Evidence, Research/Odds versions, failed attempts, consumption records and Human words. ## Modularity First — composable capabilities and safe exit

**Human-adopted on 2026-10-10, alongside Reuse First:** [adoption and scope](https://github.com/auguspp/decision-kernel/issues/508#issuecomment-6097686457), from the [modular-capability requirement](https://github.com/auguspp/decision-kernel/issues/508#issuecomment-6097504982). This is a standing project engineering principle, including after Kernel 2.0, not adoption of a particular architecture, dependency or P4 implementation scope.

**Keep a stable semantic and authority core; connect independently evolvable implementations through explicit capability contracts and composition points.** Adding, replacing, disabling or retiring a capability should change that capability and its legitimate integration points, not unrelated business internals. Unrelated functions must remain usable and lawful history recoverable after an optional implementation exits. Stability protects proven semantics, permissions and published contracts, not current filenames, directories or implementations. Domain identity, PIT, UNKNOWN, Evidence/Research/Odds and Human Authority are not freely unloadable policy plugins.

**Boundary and composition.** Distinguish capability definition, implementation provider, actual consumer and composition owner. For a material seam, identify required versus optional dependencies, inputs/outputs, state and budget ownership, failure handling, execution effects, source/unit/time constraints and supported historical versions. Event opportunities, qualified data dependencies and execution authority are different edges. Consumers should use the supported contract rather than a provider's private internals. Interfaces follow demonstrated replacement or isolation needs; do not create an interface, factory or manifest for every function, hide dependencies in a service locator, or introduce a second configuration/state authority.

**Local change, not zero change.** Adding a capability may legitimately require its contract, composition/configuration, adapter, tests and affected presentation. Do not impose an arbitrary file-count limit or pretend new semantics need no consumer work. Replacement requires equivalent supported meaning and qualification, not matching tickers or JSON fields; adapt or reject differences in provenance, clocks, units and rights explicitly. A research-method switch does not silently alter generic commit or inherit Human acceptance. Missing required capability blocks its dependent action; optional absence/failure preserves unrelated work and an explicit unavailable/partial/UNKNOWN outcome in the existing vocabulary, never fabricated quiet, zero, success or freshness.

**Four distinct exits.** Design and verify runtime disablement, provider replacement, legacy execution-entry retirement and historical-reader retirement separately. Trace actual manual, dynamic, CLI, workflow, job/step/path and configuration consumers before removing an obligation; UNKNOWN is not no-consumer evidence. Remove only the capability's obsolete registrations, listeners, future triggers, configuration and exclusive permissions/resources, preserving shared duties. Reconcile in-flight or uncertain effects under the original protocol: unregistering an in-process handler does not undo source requests, Git writes, consumed budgets or durable facts, and does not authorize automatic cancellation or retry. Preserve original bytes, failures, method/schema interpretation, lawful readers and Human records. Retire a necessary historical reader only after equivalent recovery evidence and applicable adoption; preserving historical reading does not require keeping an obsolete producer running forever.

**Reuse and modularity are coequal.** Reuse First asks whose implementation should own a responsibility; Modularity First asks how that implementation enters, composes, changes and exits. Compare explicit existing composition, native interfaces/construction/configuration, thin adaptation of maintained components and a fuller capability framework under the same present-use constraints and lifecycle costs. Static composition can be sufficient; dynamic loading or hot-unload needs a demonstrated benefit. An external architecture is not excluded merely by language or packaging, nor preferred merely because it advertises plugins. DeepSeek Harness/Cordis remain prior-art candidates under the linked requirement, not automatically adopted dependencies. No bespoke plugin runtime, event bus, scheduler or enforcement framework merely to satisfy this principle.

**Verification and net benefit.** In the existing task/PR, reuse applicable contract tests, real-consumer wiring tests and bounded end-to-end rejection/recovery samples. Where relevant, demonstrate source addition/replacement, optional capability disablement, a justified old-entry exit, research-method replacement and presentation replacement without unauthorized live calls. Compare actual integration points, change propagation, retained adapters/readers, source/Git requests, tests/CI, upgrades and recovery/exit costs. Mark static reasoning DESIGN_ESTIMATE and only actual controlled execution MEASURED. More modules, a cleaner tree, an acyclic import graph, successful registration or green CI alone is not success; old responsibility must genuinely leave where retirement is claimed. No new scoring system, parallel capability registry, permanent testing platform or whole-repository audit for routine changes.

**Ownership, retrieval and revision.** RM owns scope and acceptance; Main Construction and future maintainers apply both principles when introducing, replacing, disabling or retiring capabilities or materially changing their boundaries. AGENTS routes here; #508 retains the adoption and current architecture/handoff, while #297 retains project routing. Apply proportionately through the existing matrix, migration plan and acceptance record, not a new progress system. New evidence or Human direction can revise this principle through Reconcile/Governance with explicit replacement and preserved history; it does not expire automatically. Principle adoption, document merge, technical design adoption and demonstrated modularity remain separate. No new dependency installation, source/model execution, privacy egress, spending, permission, schedule, production migration, bulk deletion or investment authority is granted.

## Reuse First — required external prior-art check

**2026-10-01 Human-authorized clarification:** [scope and rationale](https://github.com/auguspp/decision-kernel/issues/508#issuecomment-5926750116). The project is for the Human's personal use. **Search mature GitHub/open-source solutions before committing to custom architecture or implementation, not after most of the code has been written.** Internal familiarity and official SDK availability do not replace this external check. This revises the earlier internal → official → external ordering, not the need to cover all three layers.

1. **Recover the exact need and internal reuse** — inspect current exact-`main` modules, workflows, validators, adapters, schemas, utilities and relevant #508/#511 evidence; distinguish an actual missing capability from completed or in-flight work.
2. **Actively inspect public GitHub / mature open-source solutions before custom design** — search the actual capability, including usable tools and components rather than only repositories with the same product name. Read relevant implementation and compare practical reuse options before proposing an owned subsystem.
3. **Verify official ecosystem contracts alongside candidate evaluation** — inspect relevant official SDKs, APIs, CLIs, GitHub Actions, standards and source/data terms. These checks may be interleaved with external discovery; they are not a reason to postpone or omit it.

**Retained evidence is a starting point, not a closed whitelist.** Recover #508 High-Relevance Living Prior Art and #511 candidate records first, then perform bounded fresh discovery for a new/materially changed capability or an expanded eligible candidate range. Do not restrict consideration to named reference projects or treat an old negative verdict as permanent. A prior audit can satisfy the check when its exact capability, candidate version, actual use and constraints still apply; explicitly identify that evidence and why no fresh search is needed. Material upstream changes or newly eligible personal-use options require a scoped update, not a whole-framework audit. Record the entries and search scope actually inspected. Unreadable records or failed searches are retrieval gaps, not no-match evidence.

**Broader personal-use candidate range.** Consider mature libraries, CLI tools, standalone processes, self-hosted applications, focused scripts/components, maintained forks and small proven personal projects. A tool need not be an enterprise platform, have an SLA, support multi-tenancy, match this repository's language, or expose an embeddable SDK to be reusable. Low stars, a single maintainer, or a stable release with few recent commits is not an automatic rejection; inspect actual fitness, reproducibility, maintenance/security risks and a practical exit path. Immature experiments may remain reference/candidate evidence without being adopted as production dependencies.

**Use the least costly viable boundary.** Compare direct use, configuration, a CLI/process or file/API boundary, a thin adapter, and a narrowly maintained fork/patch before rewriting. Reusing a mature application or one of its components does not require adopting its entire Agent/DAG, database, scoring, trading or governance architecture. A rejected whole project may still contribute reusable algorithms, failure modes, tests, data contracts or negative evidence. Preserve Kernel-specific epistemic, PIT, identity and Human-authority semantics at the boundary; do not demand that an external project already implements all of Kernel.

**Present-use first / no hypothetical-future tax.** Optimize for this single-user project's current real use, not speculative commercialization, proprietary redistribution, public SaaS, future scale or universal portability. Do not reject a working tool merely because a hypothetical future business model would dislike its licence or hosting model. GPL/AGPL and other copyleft projects are eligible for evaluation, not blanket exclusions. Separately labelled source-available, personal-use or noncommercial offerings can also be considered when their actual terms permit the intended use; do not call them open source by default or assume personal investing automatically satisfies a licence's noncommercial definition.

**Personal use is not a waiver of present obligations.** Check the exact version/licence and actual mode of use: local execution, modification, linking, copying into this public repository, redistribution and network hosting are different situations. Publicly visible code with no licence is not blanket reuse permission. Preserve required notices and comply with applicable source/distribution/network-service obligations; an unclear licence remains a gap. Source/API/data rights are separate from code licences. This change neither makes the public repository private nor authorizes a new project licence, paid service, credential, data egress, deployment or ongoing cost. Verify the particular candidate rather than imposing a blanket ban or exemption. Primary guidance: [GitHub licensing](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository), [GNU GPL private modifications](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html#GPLRequireSourcePostedPublic), [GNU AGPL network interaction](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html#UnreleasedModsAGPL).

Reuse the existing short record; no exhaustive candidate quota or new registry. A README, repository name, star count or marketing description alone is not adoption evidence. For a potential runtime/dependency, inspect its real entry point, relevant code and tests or formal contract where practicable; unrun validation stays NOT RUN.
