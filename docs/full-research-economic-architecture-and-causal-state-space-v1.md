# Full Research Economic Architecture & Causal State Space v1

Status: **REUSABLE FULL RESEARCH DISCIPLINE / CURRENT GENERAL METHOD / NO SCHEMA OR RUNTIME CHANGE**  
Scope: **decision-use Full Research, valuation worlds, Price-Implied Worlds and first-entry work**  
Authority: **Research method only. Investment Authority = NONE.**

## Why this exists

Several real dogfood cases exposed a common weakness that is deeper than source coverage or arithmetic:

- 恒瑞：Research did not push early enough from reported growth to owner cash after all necessary costs and reinvestment;
- 光电：operating and valuation bridges were weak enough that historical price and narrative could contaminate the valuation frame;
- 兴业科技：the first world model did not first establish the company’s economic architecture, so it naturally compressed a legacy-business decline and a new-business transition into overly tidy whole-company worlds.

The reusable failure class is:

```text
CAUSAL_STATE_SPACE_CONSTRUCTION_FAILURE
```

The lesson is not “always add one more scenario.” The deeper rule is:

> **Any decision-use world model must first demonstrate that its Economic Decomposition is fit for purpose.**

Do not ask first:

> What are Bear / Base / Bull?

Ask first:

- What are the economically distinct engines?
- What variables determine each engine’s owner economics?
- Which relationships couple those engines?
- Which load-bearing variables can move independently?

Only then construct joint worlds.

This applies well beyond explicit corporate transformations. It can matter when old products shrink while new products grow, domestic and overseas economics diverge, mix shifts between low- and high-margin products, a mature cash cow funds a capital-heavy second curve, To-C and To-B businesses move differently, customer groups diverge, or raw-material and downstream processing economics separate.

## 1. Economic Architecture before world construction

Before building decision-use worlds, identify the minimum set of economically distinct engines needed to explain owner outcomes.

Do **not** over-segment merely because accounting disclosures have many lines. Create a separate engine only when a difference can materially change at least one of:

- revenue formation;
- gross / operating margin;
- cash conversion;
- capital intensity or working-capital needs;
- reinvestment runway and incremental ROIC;
- customer / supplier bargaining power;
- cycle sensitivity;
- funding / dilution needs;
- terminal duration or risk.

For each material engine, establish a bounded economic description:

```text
volume / users / capacity
× price / ASP / take rate
→ revenue
→ gross economics
→ operating costs
→ operating / owner earnings
→ working capital + capex + other reinvestment
→ owner cash
→ incremental ROIC / value creation
```

UNKNOWN is acceptable. A clean segment table without economic causality is not enough.

## 2. Map relationships between engines

Segments do not necessarily add independently. Before SOTP or joint worlds, map material cross-engine relationships.

At least consider, where relevant:

- substitution / cannibalization;
- shared factories, channels, R&D, salesforce or fixed costs;
- stranded fixed costs when one engine shrinks;
- cross-subsidy and capital migration;
- common customers or suppliers;
- working-capital interactions;
- financing constraints;
- capacity transfer or duplication;
- management attention / execution bottlenecks;
- whether one engine is funding another before the latter becomes self-funding.

The question is not only:

> What is each segment worth?

It is first:

> **How does the economic state of one engine change the economics, funding and owner cash of the others?**

SOTP is downstream from this relationship map, not a substitute for it.

## 3. Derive independent causal axes before naming worlds

A world model should be generated from load-bearing variables that can move independently, not from three prose labels called pessimistic / neutral / optimistic.

Examples of independent axes can include:

```text
legacy demand / economics
new-business commercialization
price / mix
cost position
capacity utilization
customer concentration / share
industry supply response
capital intensity / working capital
funding / dilution
governance / evidence integrity
```

Only use axes that are material to the researched company. Do not force every company into the same template.

Then test economically plausible combinations, including anti-diagonal states where relevant:

```text
A deteriorates + B succeeds
A stabilizes + B fails
revenue rises + margin falls
profit rises + owner cash worsens
new business succeeds + capital returns remain poor
strong market expression + weak owner economics
```

A neat diagonal table is a warning sign when the underlying drivers are capable of diverging.

## 4. Close consolidated owner economics

World construction is not decision-useful until the engine-level states reconnect to consolidated owner economics.

At minimum, explain the bridge:

```text
engine revenue / economics
→ consolidated operating profit
→ parent / normalized earnings
→ operating cash flow
→ maintenance + growth reinvestment
→ financing / dilution where relevant
→ owner cash
→ incremental / consolidated ROIC
```

This strengthens a repeated weak area in prior Full Research: revenue and earnings can look better while owner economics worsen.

Required challenges include, where material:

- growth bought with disproportionate capex;
- inventory / receivables absorbing cash;
- acquisition accounting masking poor incremental returns;
- legacy cash funding a new engine for longer than assumed;
- apparent profit growth without self-funding economics;
- share issuance / debt changing per-share owner outcomes.

A successful product, order or revenue line is not automatically a successful capital-allocation outcome.

## 5. Strengthen cycle, competition and value-chain reasoning when load-bearing

Company Research should not stop at company disclosures when industry structure determines the economics.

For each load-bearing engine, ask only the relevant questions:

### Cycle

- What variable usually turns the economics: demand, price, inventory, utilization, capacity additions, input costs, credit, policy, or something else?
- Which current observations are structural and which may be cyclical?
- What would distinguish recovery from temporary restocking / price movement?

### Competition

- What competitor action can destroy the current margin / share assumption?
- Is capacity entering or exiting?
- What evidence supports durable differentiation rather than temporary scarcity?
- Does the company have pricing power, switching costs, cost advantage, qualification barriers, or merely current demand strength?

### Value chain

- Where does bargaining power sit with customers and suppliers?
- Who captures incremental economics when end demand rises?
- Can input-cost inflation be passed through, with what lag?
- Are customers concentrated enough to change price, working capital or capex economics?

Use peers and industry evidence to establish feasibility, scale, base rates and failure patterns. Do not transfer peer success probability to the researched company.

## 6. Adversarially challenge the causal state space

Adversarial Research should attack the **structure of the model**, not merely append a risk list.

Before decision-use valuation, ask:

- What load-bearing variable did the current decomposition omit?
- Which two variables have been incorrectly forced to move together?
- What plausible world preserves headline growth while damaging owner cash?
- What plausible world makes the new business commercially real but economically unattractive?
- What evidence would force a different Reference Frame, not merely a lower number inside the same frame?
- Which company statement is being treated as sustainable economics without an independent bridge?

When multiple Reference Frames remain plausible, preserve them as competing explanations and state the discriminating Evidence. Do not collapse uncertainty merely to produce a single valuation.

## 7. Valuation comes after economic architecture

Only after the causal state space and owner-economics bridge are explicit should Research build valuation worlds or first-entry conditions.

For each material world:

1. establish the earnings / owner-cash outcome;
2. establish duration, reinvestment and ROIC;
3. independently justify the valuation method / multiple / discount rate;
4. connect current price to the required economic world;
5. identify what is already proven versus what the price still requires.

Do not let one favorable story do double duty:

```text
new business succeeds
→ higher earnings
→ therefore automatically higher terminal P/E
```

The second arrow requires separate support through remaining growth duration, reinvestment returns, competitive durability, cash conversion or lower risk.

Reuse `docs/full-research-review-gate-v1.md` and `docs/full-research-price-implied-economics-closure-v1.md` for the valuation and reverse-price gates. This document is the upstream economic-architecture gate; it does not replace them.

## 8. Five mandatory closures for decision-use Full Research

A Full Research can use different industries, data sources and valuation methods, but before a numerical Odds / first-entry surface is called method-ready it should close or explicitly bound five questions:

### Closure A — Economic decomposition

What economically distinct engines actually determine owner outcomes, and why is this decomposition sufficient without gratuitous segmentation?

### Closure B — Causal relationships and independent axes

How do the engines interact, and which load-bearing variables can move independently?

### Closure C — Owner economics

How do the engine states reach consolidated earnings, cash, reinvestment, financing and ROIC?

### Closure D — Adversarial worlds

Which plausible non-diagonal or alternative Reference Frame most threatens the neat thesis? What Evidence distinguishes it?

### Closure E — Valuation bridge

Why do the resulting owner economics deserve the chosen valuation, and what exact economic requirements are embedded in the current price / candidate first-entry price?

If one closure is materially unresolved, retain the gap explicitly. The valid output can be:

```text
BOUNDED CONDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS
RESEARCH CHALLENGE — REUNDERWRITE
FIRST ENTRY = NOT METHOD-READY
CARDINAL PROBABILITY = NOT ESTABLISHED
```

Do not manufacture precision merely because the user requested a number.

## 9. Postmortem classification for future dogfood

When Human review exposes a missed load-bearing issue, classify it before changing the method:

```text
METHOD GAP
The method did not require the question.

EXECUTION GAP
The method required it, but the Research did not execute it adequately.

SOURCE GAP
The right question was identified but admissible evidence was unavailable / incomplete.

JUDGMENT GAP
The relevant facts existed, but the causal or valuation interpretation was wrong.
```

Do not respond to every execution miss by adding another mandatory checklist item. Expand the general method only when repeated real failures establish a reusable gap.

A useful quality metric is:

> **After Full Research, how many load-bearing questions does the Human still have to discover that the Research should reasonably have asked itself?**

Human disagreement with judgment is normal. Repeated Human discovery of previously unasked load-bearing questions is a Research-quality failure signal.

## 10. Relationship to the Xingye transition-company retrospective

`docs/readings/transition-company-full-research-lessons-2026-09-14.md` is retained as the historical, case-specific predecessor earned from the 兴业科技 failure.

Its useful case lessons remain valid — legacy value can decay before revenue, anti-diagonal worlds matter, acquisition reality is not scale economics, capital migration matters, terminal P/E needs its own bridge, and numerical coincidence does not validate a wrong model.

The present document generalizes the deeper lesson:

> **The problem was not merely a missing transition scenario. The Research had not first constructed the correct economic architecture and causal state space.**

For current general decision-use Full Research, use this document first. Use the transition-company note as an origin-case addendum when the case actually contains a legacy-to-new-business handover.

## 11. Boundaries

This discipline does not create:

- a new Kernel schema;
- a fixed segment taxonomy;
- a universal SOTP method;
- a mandatory Bear/Base/Bull table;
- a probability engine;
- an Agent / DAG / provider framework;
- a new Research workflow;
- monitoring, trading or Investment Authority.

It strengthens Research reasoning before worlds are converted into valuation and Odds. Requirements Management still owns prioritization; Main Construction still owns runtime implementation. Evidence changes Belief. Price changes Odds. UNKNOWN remains allowed.
