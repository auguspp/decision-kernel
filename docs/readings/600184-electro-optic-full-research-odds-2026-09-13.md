## 7. Method correction / lesson retained

The first pass produced an excessively low first-entry region because the method mixed two separate operations:

1. defining economic worlds / Belief; and
2. demanding very strong downside protection / Odds.

That implicitly pushed a high margin-of-safety preference into the world set itself.

The corrected discipline is:

1. **Reality Map first.** What the company has actually demonstrated.
2. **Market Belief Map second.** Include sell-side earnings dispersion, historical extreme pricing and what the market has demonstrably been willing to capitalize.
3. **Our Belief third.** Define worlds using operating evidence, historical economics, capacity/margin constraints and external expectation anchors. Do not use seller forecasts as truth, but do not omit them from expectation mapping.
4. **Only then Price → Odds.** Margin-of-safety requirements may change entry price; they must not silently redefine the company's ordinary/core operating world.
5. **Separate `RE_UNDERWRITE`, `FIRST_ENTRY_REVIEW`, and `HIGH_MOS`.** A high-MOS price is not automatically the correct default first-entry price.
6. **Historical high price is an expectation datapoint, not a valuation anchor.** CNY33.55 explains what right-tail expectations were once capitalized; it does not validate intrinsic value.
