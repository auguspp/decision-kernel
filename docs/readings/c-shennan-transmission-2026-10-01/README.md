## Findings and the three challenged inferences

1. **Industry → company.** [Issuer report pp12–13](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-27/1225512708.PDF#page=13) reports data-center-related PCB revenue exceeding CNY2bn. That is issuer-reported exposure, not an independently identified AI causal effect, exact share or profit contribution. Material shortages, cost pressure and customer ordering brought forward remain alternative mechanisms.
2. **Company → profit.** p16/p127 portfolio gross profit totals CNY4,744,152,836.80; PCB supplies 66.4368%, mixed packaging substrates 24.2295%. Group gross-profit growth is CNY1,996,526,762.63. **“All group growth is BGA” is unsupported, not numerically disproved by current-period shares.** Exact prior product costs and pure-FC-BGA splits are missing. Successful group economics cannot be transferred to Xingsen's product probability or unit economics.
3. **Legal entity ≠ factory.** p21/p22 Guangzhou Guangxin net profit of 52,623.14万元 includes 39,000万元 intercompany-dividend investment income, or 74.1119%. Thus **“subsidiary profit is factory operating profit” is invalid**. The CNY136,231,400 subtraction is only a diagnostic, not normalized plant profit. The dividend is already eliminated in consolidated accounts: neither subtract it again from group profit nor count Wuxi and Guangzhou independently.
4. **Profit → cash → capital.** Both p133 17-line bridges close. Consolidated net-profit growth CNY893,302,262.88 plus other adjustments CNY579,533,171.14 less additional working-capital absorption CNY1,466,505,889.77 yields only CNY6,329,544.25 more CFO. H1 CFO CNY1,501,855,888.16 less p53 cash capex CNY4,678,636,816.28 is **−CNY3,176,780,928.12** (prior H1 −CNY50,403,396.15). **“Positive CFO means expansion self-funds” fails this period's cash test.** Financing is separate; this gap is not FCFE, maintenance capex, investment NPV or evidence of value destruction.
