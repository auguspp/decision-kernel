### B. Procedure monetization

- installed-base utilization and procedure volume;

- attach rate and recurring-revenue share;
- gross margin and contribution economics tied to procedure growth.

### C. Owner-cash conversion

- revenue growth re-accelerates with procedure growth;
- operating loss narrows for economic rather than accounting-only reasons;
- operating cash flow materially improves;
- working-capital / sales-channel economics become more supportive.

### D. Transaction economics

- final valuation and consideration for the proposed 62% Shanghai MicroPort Orthopedics acquisition;
- number of purchase shares issued and any matching financing dilution;
- audited target-company financials and debt / liability structure;
- goodwill and pro-forma balance-sheet consequences;
- post-deal earnings, cash-flow and per-share economics.

### E. Reference-Frame evidence

- credible evidence that robot + implant + overseas-channel integration changes unit economics or customer acquisition economics;
- or evidence that the transaction merely combines two loss-making / capital-intensive businesses without improving owner returns.
