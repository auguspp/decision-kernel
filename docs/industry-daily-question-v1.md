## What changes and what stays

The original daily request, workflow mode string and host remain. The old
BOUNDED_DAILY_REVIEWED_STOCK_QUESTION name is transport compatibility, not an
assertion that Industry passed a Stock price gate. An Industry request instead
binds the new exact permission and DAILY_INDUSTRY_BATCH_REVIEW purpose, checked
against industry-daily-question-extension.json at the same main commit.
The original authorize function rechecks the actual comment ID/body hash/date,
trusted main and exact request before admission and every model stage.

stock_daily_question.bind_custody is a direct extraction of its original public
PDF byte/page/inventory/journal checks; Stock and Industry call the same function.
No permissive clone or fake Stock candidate is supplied. Original Stock policy,
permission, active already-consumed Zhenjiang request and historical bytes stay
unchanged. This change activates no new Industry company request or ready label.

Industry inputs must bind the whole saved native breadth report in one pinned R,
its exact run and complete artifact inventory, the original ZIP and capture
replay. Review all returned rows, including invalid rows, in source order with
row content hashes and explicit reasons. There must be at least one selected,
dated, non-financial Industry row. An accountable economic-exposure declaration
binds the same company and retained public company context, mechanism,
materiality basis, counterevidence and falsification test. This checks identity
and declared scope, not economic truth or quantified business benefit.

The first adapter uses the same-R qualified Sector completed-market-day record
and verifies its original run identity as a shared A-share day anchor. It does
NOT require Sector strength, company membership or any Stock price gate. This
is an explicit remaining day-anchor dependency, not an independent futures
calendar. Capture time is not a market date. Selected spot source dates must
match that completed day; no claim follows about continuous-contract close
finality, units, tax, roll composition or company-contract equivalence.

for repeated approval. The Human reports an account-side limit; the application
does not verify that claim or assert infinite service capacity. No new monetary
ceiling, balance poll or cheap-model fallback is installed.

consumption arrangement and October20 expiry with Stock. It is not a separate
Industry quota, and prior failed/consumed slots/days/question roots remain
binding. Model/transport timeouts, complete-source byte/page limits, safe egress,
no automatic retry/Deep and Human investment authority are unaffected by money.

permission into reservations, so old markers remain readable and counted.

preflight/prepare/admission checks must establish the declared update inventory
and necessary primary reads; the Industry adapter does not construct or approve
