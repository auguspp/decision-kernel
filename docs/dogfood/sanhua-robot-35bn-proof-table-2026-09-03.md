## 0. Purpose

This file turns the familiar sell-side success-state arithmetic

```text
1.0m annual robot units
× CNY50,000 actuator value / robot
× 70% Sanhua share
× 10% net margin
= CNY3.5bn / 35亿元 profit
```

into a proof table.

The question is not whether the multiplication is correct.

The question is:

> What must be true in the real industrial system for Sanhua to earn CNY3.5bn of durable robot profit, and which of those conditions are actually established by public evidence today?

This remains a Research challenger. It does not modify frozen Research, Human Decision, Action, or the accepted Xiaohongshu publication draft.

## 2. The cross-state assumption problem

The most important case-specific failure currently identified is not arithmetic error. It is **state mixing**.

Dongwu's own framing contains two different commercialization states:

```text
early ramp:
value content = CNY200k+ / robot
share ≈ higher / ~70% headline case

large-scale maturity:
value content ≈ CNY50k / robot
share may decline toward ≈50%
```

The headline 35亿元 state uses:

```text
mature low ASP
×
higher share
```

without an independent reason that high share persists after mass-production sourcing, cost-down and second-source qualification.

### State-consistency derivations

Holding units and net margin constant:

```text
1.0m × CNY50k × 50% × 10%
= CNY2.5bn
```

To recover CNY3.5bn at 50% mature share, one compensating variable must move:

```text
1.4m units × CNY50k × 50% × 10%
= CNY3.5bn
```

or

```text
1.0m units × CNY70k × 50% × 10%
= CNY3.5bn
```

or

```text
1.0m units × CNY50k × 50% × 14%
= CNY3.5bn
```

These are `DERIVATION`, not forecasts.

This failure is preserved as a Sanhua case finding only. It is **not** promoted into a new generic Kernel schema or method gate.

## 3. BOM / manufacturing reference-class evidence

### 3.1 Tuopu robot actuator: realized early-ramp economics

**FACT** — Tuopu 2025 annual report:

```text
robot actuator revenue = CNY13.591m
robot actuator cost = CNY9.752m
gross margin = 28.25%
revenue YoY = +1.22%
cost YoY = +47.92%
gross margin YoY = -22.65ppt
```

Its disclosed robot-actuator cost components were:

```text
direct material = CNY5.928m
direct labor = CNY1.651m
manufacturing expense = CNY2.173m
```

**DERIVATION** — as a share of disclosed actuator cost:

```text
material ≈ 60.8%
labor ≈ 16.9%
manufacturing expense ≈ 22.3%
```

This is not Sanhua's BOM. It is useful because it shows that an integrated actuator business can carry meaningful purchased-material burden **and** meaningful labor / manufacturing absorption at the same time.

Tuopu 2026H1:

```text
robot actuator revenue = CNY14.048m
robot actuator cost = CNY10.250m
gross margin = 27.03%
revenue YoY = +83.43%
cost YoY = +92.75%
```

**INFERENCE** — strong revenue growth during ramp does not guarantee gross-margin expansion.

### 3.2 Leaderdrive: precision component expansion is capital- and utilization-sensitive

**FACT** — 2025 industrial + embodied-intelligence robot-parts revenue was CNY422.5m with 34.88% GM.

**FACT** — its new precision-transmission project plans:

```text
total investment = CNY2.030bn
equipment purchase + installation = CNY1.609bn / 79.25%
initial working capital = CNY303.8m / 14.96%
planned new capacity:
  1.0m harmonic reducers/year
  0.2m mechatronic products/year
```

**MARKET_CONTEXT / MANAGEMENT MODEL** — the original project economics assumed:

```text
construction = 2 years
ramp utilization after construction = 20% / 60% / 100%
ASP decline:
  harmonic reducer = -3%/year
  mechatronic product = -5%/year
```

**FACT — realized outside view** — later utilization did not follow a frictionless straight line:

```text
2023 theoretical utilization = 51.59%
2024 = 42.67%
2025 = 67.76%
```

The company delayed the newer project from end-2026 to end-2028 partly because prior capacity utilization had been low and it chose to absorb existing capacity first.

### 3.3 Nabtesco / Harmonic Drive Systems: utilization matters even for mature leaders

**FACT** — Nabtesco FY2024Q4 disclosed:

```text
Tsu precision-reducer plant utilization = 65%
China plant utilization = 100%
```

Nabtesco repeatedly attributes precision-reducer profit recovery to higher utilization, price pass-through, cost reduction and automation; its 2024 Q1 CMP operating margin fell to 3.6% during weak utilization.

**FACT** — Harmonic Drive Systems FY2025/26 reported material operating-profit recovery and explicitly cited price revision, cost innovation and higher Japan-factory utilization as drivers.

**Outside-view conclusion:**

```text
capacity built
!= capacity utilized
!= yield mature
!= ASP stable
!= gross margin mature
!= owner return mature
```

## 4. Second-source / allocation evidence

### 4.1 What can be said from customer-side public evidence

Tesla's 2025 Form 10-K states that:

- some components and systems are single-sourced;
- where multiple sources exist for key components, Tesla works to qualify multiple suppliers where sensible to reduce production-disruption risk;
- Tesla continuously negotiates supplier cost reductions;
- it seeks lower-cost suppliers and redesigns parts to reduce cost.

This is `MARKET_CONTEXT` for the likely downstream procurement environment, **not proof of an Optimus-specific dual-source mandate and not proof that Sanhua supplies Tesla**.

### 4.2 What can be said from supplier-side public evidence

**FACT** — Sanhua says robot actuator products are in batch delivery / line ramp but does not publicly identify customer allocation.

**FACT** — Tuopu has a real robot-actuator revenue line and therefore represents observable competing actuator capability.

**FACT** — Sanhua / Leaderdrive contemplated a Leaderdrive-controlled Mexico JV for harmonic reducers; a later Sanhua investor response said cooperation progress was slow and the factory had not formally reached mass production at that point.

**NOT ESTABLISHED:**

```text
customer-specific supplier list
formal customer-specific qualified-source status
mandatory second source by actuator type
Sanhua vs Tuopu allocation
rotary vs linear split
overlap vs complementarity
long-run 70% economic share
```

## 5. Capital / owner-economics bridge that must eventually close

The eventual Research bridge should be:

```text
terminal robot units
× Sanhua economic allocation
× mature billable content
= robot revenue

robot revenue
- purchased content
- direct materials
- labor
- manufacturing depreciation / overhead
- scrap / rework / warranty
= gross profit

- robot-attributable R&D
- SG&A / logistics / customer support
- taxes
= NOPAT / normalized owner profit

then:

realized robot development capital
+ pilot / validation / test / tooling capital
+ robot receivables
+ robot inventory
- robot payables
+ robot PP&E
= incremental invested capital

incremental NOPAT
/
incremental invested capital
= incremental ROIC

incremental NOPAT

- maintenance capex
- change in working capital
```

Current public evidence cannot fill this bridge with defensible Sanhua-specific numbers.

## 6. What evidence would materially upgrade the Research

Highest-value next evidence, in order:

1. **Customer / supplier allocation evidence**
   - direct customer filing / IR / technical document;
   - supplier confirmation of qualified-source status;
   - rotary / linear / hand actuator allocation;
   - second-source or requalification evidence.

2. **Actual commercial price / content evidence**
   - actuator module quotation / procurement disclosures;
   - component count and module architecture;
   - year-over-year customer price-down;
   - evidence of architecture changes reducing content.

3. **Robot-specific manufacturing evidence**
   - rated line capacity;
   - equipment / line count;
   - yield / first-pass yield;
   - cycle time / automation;
   - utilization / actual shipments.

4. **Robot-specific capital evidence**
   - realized development / R&D cash burden after the terminated GDR plan;
   - fixed assets / tooling / equipment assigned to robot;
   - working capital;
   - maintenance capex.

5. **Realized margin evidence**
   - robot revenue and gross profit disclosure;
   - segment / product contribution margin;
   - peer realized economics through a full ramp cycle.

Until these are available, a tighter-looking numerical model would mostly hide unknowns rather than reduce them.

## 8. Current Research verdict

The 35亿元 state is **not disproven**, but it is not independently established.

The strongest current challenge is:

```text
1m downstream planned capacity
!= 1m realized robot output

batch delivery / line ramp
!= customer-specific durable allocation

CNY50k mature billable content
!= CNY50k Sanhua value added

70% early / headline share
!= 70% long-run mass-production allocation

10% net margin scenario
!= 10% realized owner margin

planned development budget
!= realized robot invested capital

35亿元 accounting profit
!= 35亿元 owner cash
```

The research has therefore advanced from:

```text
sell-side gives a 35亿元 success scenario
```

one step closer to:

```text
we can identify the industrial conditions required for 35亿元,
which conditions are currently supported,
which are internally inconsistent across commercialization states,
and which missing capital / allocation / margin facts still prevent owner-economics closure.
```

The next upgrade should come from **new evidence**, not a tighter-looking guessed model.
