## Material changes from initial draft to published copy

### 1. The opening moved from 2025 backward-looking PE to 2026 current reality

Initial accepted draft opened:

```text
42.93元。
2025年，恒瑞医药归母净利润77.11亿元，EPS 1.19元。
按这个利润算，42.93元对应大约36倍PE。
```

The Human later questioned why the 2025 number deserved to lead the article.

Published copy instead opens with:

```text
2026年上半年，恒瑞医药归母净利润44.65亿元，扣非净利润37.30亿元。
这个半年不能直接乘2。
...
42.67元这个价格，已经要求恒瑞赚多少钱。
```

Then 2025 appears only as a **complete-year reference** after the implied-profit table.

Lesson:

> A historical full-year number may be useful as a reference, but it should not lead unless it directly answers the headline question better than the latest operating reality.

### 2. The current-price question moved ahead of the historical reference

The published copy makes the reader confront CNY42.67 first, then asks what profit that price requires at 36x / 30x / 24x.

Only after that does it introduce CNY77.11bn 2025 profit as an anchor.

This is cleaner because the historical number now has a defined job:

> `last full-year actual reference`, not `current normalized earnings`.

### 3. The published copy became shorter and more assertive

Examples of compression that survived Human editing:

- `这个转型是真的。` became simply `转型是真的。`
- `所以研发成果有没有外部价值，这件事已经不用争。` became `这件事已经没有争议。`
- `不算离谱，但也看不出便宜。` became `不算离谱，但也还没到捡便宜的程度。`

The published tone is slightly more ordinary and less analytical-memo-like.

Lesson:

> Once the causal chain is clear, some qualifiers can be shortened. But the economic object must remain explicit.

### 4. The final close was shortened

The initial draft ended with a longer list of what investors should watch.

The published copy ends earlier:

```text

42.67元的问题是，这个机会已经被计入到价格里了。
```

This is a stronger publication ending because it returns directly to the title and does not reopen the research checklist.

Lesson:

> A research note may end with monitoring variables. A public article can end earlier once the headline question is closed.

General rule:

> Before keeping a number in publication, ask: `Why does the reader need this number at this exact point?`

If the answer is merely `because it is useful context` or `because Research found it`, move it later or delete it.

## Final calibration

For future price-led articles, the initial-draft gate is not enough. After the structure is coherent, run one more editorial check:

1. Does every early number have a clear role in the price argument?
2. Is the latest operating reality appearing before a stale annual anchor when appropriate?
3. Can any paragraph be shortened without breaking the causal bridge?
4. Does the ending return to the headline instead of reopening a research checklist?
5. Has publication-time price context been updated without mutating frozen Research?
