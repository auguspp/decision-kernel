## 2. Correct Reference Frame

宁德时代不适合被简化成单一“锂电池周期股”，也不适合被当作低资本投入的软件式平台。

更合适的 Reference Frame 是：

```text
global scaled electrochemical manufacturing franchise
+ deep product / process R&D
+ long customer qualification and safety record
+ purchasing / supply-chain scale
+ power battery leadership
+ fast-growing energy-storage platform
+ recycling / materials integration
+ overseas local manufacturing
+ emerging zero-carbon infrastructure and services
```

它的经济优势主要来自：

```text
technology + safety + yield + scale + qualification + customer coverage
```

它的经济负担则来自：

```text
very large fixed-asset base
+ continuous chemistry / process reinvestment
+ global localization
+ working capital
+ warranty / quality obligations
+ supply-chain financing complexity
+ policy / trade fragmentation
```

所以最重要的问题不是“行业还增长吗”，而是：

> **增长能否在竞争、降价、扩产和全球本地化之后，持续转化为每股 owner earnings、owner cash 和足够高的增量资本回报。**

## 6. Principal adversarial findings

### 6.1 Growth does not eliminate price competition

EV and ESS revenue grew rapidly, but both segment gross margins declined. The company may still be gaining scale and absolute gross profit, yet the current evidence does not prove that unit economics are expanding alongside volume.

### 6.2 High utilisation today does not settle incremental ROIC

A high current utilisation rate is favorable. It does not prove that the large new domestic and overseas capacity under construction will earn the same returns after ramp cost, local labor / energy / compliance, customer concentration and future supply response.

### 6.3 Global demand runway coexists with large capacity

IEA's demand outlook is supportive, while global nameplate capacity already materially exceeds current deployment. CATL can outperform the industry through share gains and execution, but industry growth alone cannot underwrite its terminal multiple.

### 6.4 Overseas growth carries policy and localization cost

U.S. procurement / supply-chain restrictions and the Section 1260H / prohibited-foreign-entity policy direction constrain parts of the U.S. opportunity set. In Europe, battery carbon-footprint, recycling and due-diligence rules increase compliance requirements while strategic-autonomy policies encourage more local capacity.

Local production may preserve access and improve customer proximity, but increases committed capital and execution risk.

### 6.5 Financial strength can hide capital-allocation mistakes

Large cash balances and operating cash generation lower solvency risk. They do not guarantee that every plant, mine, fund investment, swap station, zero-carbon project or acquisition earns more than the cost of equity.

## 7. Price-implied economics at CNY351

Assumptions used only for reverse-underwriting:

```text
observed price = CNY351
shares = ~4.6267bn
market cap = ~CNY1.624tn
H1 attributable equity = ~CNY379.35bn
price / book = ~4.28x
2025 parent profit = CNY72.20bn
2025 adjusted parent profit = CNY64.51bn
```

### 7.1 Earnings requirement

Assume:

- a 2028 horizon, approximately two years from the observation date;
- 10% annual required return;
- no explicit dividend or excess-cash credit;
- terminal P/E as a sensitivity, not a belief.

Then:

| 2028 terminal P/E | Required 2028 EPS | Required 2028 parent profit | CAGR from 2025 parent profit |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 15x | CNY28.31 | CNY131.0bn | ~22.0% |
| 18x | CNY23.60 | CNY109.2bn | ~14.8% |
| 20x | CNY21.24 | CNY98.2bn | ~10.8% |

Dividends and genuinely excess cash would reduce the required earnings. Lower terminal multiples, execution slippage or capital consumption would raise them.

This shows that CNY351 is not pricing in impossible economics. It is pricing in continued meaningful growth and/or a durable premium multiple.

### 7.2 Long-run ROE requirement

Using the simplified residual-income identity:

```text
justified P/B ≈ (ROE - g) / (r - g)
```

and current P/B of about 4.28x:

| Cost of equity `r` | Long-run growth `g` | Implied sustainable ROE |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10% | 5% | ~26.4% |
| 11% | 5% | ~30.7% |
| 10% | 7% | ~19.8% |
| 11% | 7% | ~24.1% |

This is a steady-state sensitivity with strong assumptions, not intrinsic value. It says the current price requires CATL to preserve approximately high-teens to low-30s long-run ROE depending on how much durable growth and risk one grants.

### 7.3 What the price is asking to be true

At CNY351, the market is broadly requiring several things to coexist:

```text
global battery demand continues growing
+ CATL maintains very high share
+ EV / ESS margins do not structurally compress
+ overseas plants reach acceptable utilisation and returns
+ working-capital and supplier-finance dependence does not worsen
+ new businesses do not consume capital without owner-cash conversion
+ long-run ROE remains unusually high for a capital-intensive manufacturer
```

The public evidence strongly supports the first two. It only partially supports the remaining requirements.
