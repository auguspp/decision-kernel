## 1. Purpose

This note tests a materially different Fundamental Belief proposed by the Human:

> specialty-memory supply tightness may remain meaningful through 2028 and perhaps 2029.

The purpose is not to convert that view into a recommendation or a forced probability distribution.

The narrower question is:

> **If the shortage lasts materially longer than a normal one-year memory upcycle, how much does that change GigaDevice's owner-economics duration, and what price is compatible with a high margin of safety?**

This note therefore separates four things that must not be collapsed:

1. `shortage duration`;
2. `GigaDevice recurring earnings duration`;
3. `owner-cash conversion`;
4. `post-shortage normalized earnings / terminal economics`.

A long shortage is valuable only if it survives all four translations.

## 3. Product-by-product duration map

### A. Niche DRAM — strongest duration case

Current evidence supports a longer cycle than ordinary commodity-memory mean reversion:

```text
major suppliers prioritize HBM / advanced server memory
+ mature-node capacity crowded out
+ enterprise SSD / networking use legacy DRAM components
+ automotive / industrial qualification slows substitution
+ new fab output arrives with long construction / qualification lags
-> structural tightness can persist beyond 2027
```

But the contradiction is equally important:

```text
global DRAM capex is accelerating
+ new capacity begins phasing in from 2027
+ CXMT is itself scaling rapidly
-> 2028-2029 supply response is real, not hypothetical
```

Therefore:

> **Niche DRAM shortage through 2028 is a serious world. Shortage through all of 2029 is still a thesis, not a fact.**

### B. SLC NAND — structural niche shortage, but do not generalize broad NAND

SLC has unusually strong structural support:

- mature 2D / legacy capacity is being exited;
- industrial / automotive / networking specifications have long qualification cycles;
- MLC shortages can push substitution demand into SLC;
- major suppliers are not announcing near-term dedicated SLC capacity additions.

However, TrendForce separately expects broad NAND supply to loosen in 2H27.

The correct inference is:

> **Broad NAND easing does not automatically end SLC scarcity, but it raises substitution / allocation risk and prevents us from treating the entire NAND complex as structurally short through 2029.**

### C. NOR Flash — duration depends on density and qualification

High-density automotive / edge-AI NOR has better structural support than low-density generic NOR.

Chinese supply additions can stabilize lower-density pricing earlier.

Therefore NOR must not be modeled as one homogeneous shortage factor.

### D. MCU / custom memory — the normalization floor, not the shortage proof

MCU evidence currently supports several quarters of tightness, not a 2029 shortage claim.

Its strategic importance is different:

> **MCU, custom memory, analog and adjacent products determine how high GigaDevice's normalized earnings floor can be after specialty-memory pricing normalizes.**

This is critical because a 2029 shortage thesis is much more valuable if 2030 normalized NP is CNY16-19bn than if earnings collapse toward CNY8-11bn.

## 4. The CXMT / foundry relationship is both advantage and risk

GigaDevice is fabless and relies materially on external manufacturing.

For 2026 it expects approximately CNY5.711bn of DRAM foundry-service purchases from CXMT; H1 disclosed about CNY1.986bn purchased. Management said the full-year amount remained expected around CNY5.7bn, with the increase driven materially by higher foundry pricing as well as product / demand growth.

This relationship creates a two-sided duration effect.

### Advantage

```text
strategic foundry access
+ domestic DRAM manufacturing scale
+ GigaDevice product definition / niche-market channel
-> GigaDevice can capture a shortage without owning a fab
```

### Risk

```text
CXMT capacity expands
+ foundry input cost rises
+ GigaDevice has limited direct wafer-capacity ownership
-> gross-margin duration depends on ASP / mix / cost pass-through, not shortage alone
```

A durable shortage thesis therefore requires more than “CXMT can supply GigaDevice.”

It requires:

> **GigaDevice can secure enough wafer supply while retaining enough pricing and product-mix economics to keep owner margins structurally above the old cycle floor.**

## 7. Owner-cash treatment

The H1 realized cash conversion is unusually strong:

```text
H1 OCF ~6.048bn
- long-lived asset cash capex ~0.945bn
= ~5.103bn
vs H1 deducted NP ~4.883bn
```

The duration model does **not** annualize this >100% relationship.

Instead, it uses an intentionally conservative experimental assumption:

```text
incremental owner-cash retention / distributable-cash equivalent
= 60% of 2027-2029 NP
```

This is not a forecast. It is a valuation bridge.

Why 60%:

- below the realized H1 pre-financial-investment cash conversion;
- leaves room for working capital, capex, product investment and cycle effects;
- avoids pretending all accounting earnings instantly become distributable cash.

The model ignores existing cash as a source of upside rather than aggressively capitalizing it. It also does not give a terminal multiple to H1 non-recurring fair-value gains.

## 10. Why 2029 mark-to-market can mislead

A tempting shortcut is:

```text
2029 NP remains 18-20bn
× 20x P/E
= attractive 2029 price
```

That can overstate value if 2029 is the final year of scarcity.

For example, at current CNY388.86, a 2029 mark-to-market of 20bn NP × 20x can look attractive. But if 2030 NP then falls sharply, the 2029 multiple may itself compress before the accounting peak ends.

This creates a recognition / duration problem:

> **The market can begin discounting normalization before the shortage is visibly over.**

For a cyclical-duration thesis, the correct terminal question is not only:

> “How much does 2029 earn?”

It is:

> **“What will investors believe about 2030-2032 while we are holding the stock in 2028-2029?”**

This is why the 2030 normalized floor is first-order.

## 12. What would qualify the Human duration thesis further?

### Evidence that strengthens it

1. Specialty DRAM contract prices plateau at high levels rather than collapsing as new capacity begins to ramp.
2. GigaDevice storage gross margin remains structurally above the FY2025 baseline despite foundry-cost increases.
3. DRAM volume continues to scale through CXMT supply access without gross-margin leakage.
4. High-density NOR and SLC NAND retain pricing power even if broad NAND loosens.
5. MCU / custom-memory revenue and profit grow enough to raise the normalized post-shortage earnings floor.
6. OCF remains strong after inventory / receivable normalization; owner cash continues to track recurring earnings.
7. Fresh 2028-29 market expectations rise because of recurring operating economics, not one-off mark-to-market gains.

### Evidence that weakens it

1. DRAM new-capacity ramp causes price / margin compression materially earlier than 2028-29.
2. CXMT expands supply but captures the economics through foundry pricing, leaving GigaDevice with lower incremental margin.
3. SLC / NOR high prices trigger meaningful substitution or customer destruction.
4. Inventory or receivables rise faster than sales while OCF conversion falls.
5. MCU / custom-memory remains too small to prevent a post-cycle earnings cliff.
6. 2028 estimates roll over across a matched analyst panel before realized company evidence improves.
7. Market multiple compresses even while earnings rise because investors begin discounting the 2030 normalization cliff.
