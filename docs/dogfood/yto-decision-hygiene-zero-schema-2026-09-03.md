## Purpose

This dogfood deliberately tests whether a Research result can be mature and Human-useful while refusing to manufacture a numerical probability distribution.

It does **not**:

- change Kernel Constitution;
- add schema or enums;
- change `ResearchSnapshot`, `Scenario`, live Odds, or production policy;
- rewrite or delete YTO Full Research v2;
- change production universe;
- issue a Recommendation or Human investment decision.

The existing YTO v2 remains a frozen counterfactual baseline. Its `15/25/35/20/5` scenario distribution and subsequent live Odds remain historical artifacts. This dogfood asks whether those probabilities were methodologically qualified, not whether the deterministic arithmetic was correct.

## 3. Reference Frame battle

### Frame A — Network compounder entering harvest phase

Mechanism:

```text
scale density + digital/AI control + mature infrastructure
→ structurally lower unit cost
→ service / brand advantage
→ sustained share gains without price sacrifice
→ capex intensity falls
→ OCF converts to FCF
→ durable ROIC / owner earnings
```

Evidence supporting this frame:

- multi-year core transport / hub cost reductions;
- parcel growth above industry in 2025 and 2026H1;
- H1 unit express profit around CNY0.21/parcel;
- company says infrastructure layout is increasingly complete and future capex should trend lower;
- H1 2026 OCF exceeded cash long-term-asset purchases.

### Frame B — Policy-sensitive franchised logistics network with cyclical unit margins

Mechanism:

```text
homogeneous service + franchise bargaining constraints + periodic price competition
→ efficiency gains partially competed away
→ unit economics depend materially on industry discipline / regulation
→ HQ margin can diverge from franchise economics
→ reinvestment remains non-trivial
→ earnings durability and terminal multiple remain cyclical / policy-sensitive
```

Evidence supporting this frame:

- unit revenue fell from 2023 through H1 2026 despite major efficiency gains;
- 2025 express unit NP was about CNY0.148/parcel versus ~CNY0.206/parcel in H1 2026, so the current run-rate is a clear regime change rather than a long-established steady state;
- 2025 OCF was below cash long-term-asset purchases;
- regulator intervention explicitly focuses on franchise economics and minimum delivery cost;
- the largest H1 unit-cost decline is in pickup/delivery service cost, whose economic decomposition is not disclosed sufficiently to establish that it is pure productivity rather than mix / settlement / value-chain redistribution;
- aviation and international parcel economics do not yet support a broad high-ROIC compounder frame.

### Current frame disposition

Retain a hybrid frame:

> **Scaled franchised network with structurally improving operating efficiency, but monetization, franchise economics and owner-cash duration remain policy/cycle sensitive; transition to a mature low-reinvestment network compounder is possible but not yet proven.**

This is more useful than forcing either Frame A or Frame B as truth.

### Retrospective fit

The hybrid frame explains why YTO can simultaneously show:

- large multi-year core-cost improvement;
- rising volume / share;
- historically falling unit revenue;
- uneven unit profit;
- very heavy reinvestment through 2025;
- a sharp 2026H1 profit step-up when industry discipline improved.

### Prospective discrimination

The two frames make meaningfully different predictions over the next several quarters:

- If Frame A is increasingly correct, YTO should sustain high unit profit while share grows, franchise health remains stable, and capex/OCF falls structurally.
- If Frame B dominates, unit revenue or network economics should deteriorate again when competition/policy conditions normalize, or cash-return improvement should lag reported profit.

## 5. Causal Worlds — deliberately no probabilities

These are broad Research Worlds, not current Kernel `Scenario` objects. They carry no assigned probability and no terminal value.

### World A — Competitive relapse

```text
industry discipline weakens
→ unit revenue resumes meaningful decline
→ structural core-cost gains only partly offset price pressure
→ share may still grow, but at weaker owner economics
→ franchise pressure rises
→ unit express NP returns toward pre-2026 levels
→ cash conversion remains mediocre
```

Discriminating evidence: renewed price declines, weaker franchise economics, unit profit falling despite efficiency gains.

### World B — HQ margin looks strong but network economics weaken

```text
reported HQ unit margin remains elevated
+ pickup/delivery accounting/cost line remains favorable
but franchise economics deteriorate
→ service / network instability emerges with lag
→ future share quality and price discipline weaken
→ current margin proves less durable than reported P&L suggests
```

This world exists because HQ margin and full-network moat are not the same economic object.

Discriminating evidence: YTO-specific network churn, delivery-fee stress, service deterioration, regulatory intervention, or later need to redistribute economics back to endpoints.

### World C — Rational but cyclical network

```text
anti-involution / rational competition persists for a useful period
→ unit revenue broadly stable
→ modest structural core-cost gains continue
→ unit express profit holds near current broad range but does not compound indefinitely
→ share grows modestly
→ capex intensity falls but remains periodic
→ owner FCF improves
```

This is consistent with a better business and better industry regime without requiring a permanent compounder conclusion.

### World D — Durable network economics / harvest transition

```text
share gains are quality-led rather than price-led
+ AI/digitalization lowers true full-network cost
+ franchise economics remain healthy
+ infrastructure maturity lowers reinvestment materially
→ unit owner economics remain structurally high
→ OCF converts to durable FCF
→ incremental ROIC stays attractive
→ earnings duration and terminal economics improve
```

This world requires more than high 2026H1 profit. It requires evidence that the benefit survives through the full network and through the capital cycle.

### World coverage assessment

These four worlds appear sufficient to frame the current public-PIT debate. Additional current reading is unlikely to create a trustworthy probability measure because the main discriminants are future observations, not missing arithmetic.

This is a reconstruction from realized/company/regulatory records while deliberately excluding sell-side forecasts and price as belief inputs. It is not a claim of psychological decontamination from prior market knowledge.

No numerical probability is generated at this stage.

## 10. Probability Qualification Review

### Reference class / base rate

**Insufficient.** There is no calibrated reference class in the current Research record for Chinese franchised parcel networks transitioning from price wars into regulator-backed competition discipline while simultaneously changing capex intensity.

### Driver-state evidence

**Partial.** Current unit economics, volume/share and cost improvements are observed. Their duration and full-network ownership are not.

### World boundaries

**Sufficient for qualitative underwriting.** The current four causal worlds are distinguishable and economically coherent enough to guide monitoring.

### Dependency understanding

**Incomplete.** Key dependencies remain unresolved:

- price discipline ↔ share gain;
- HQ margin ↔ franchise economics;
- unit-cost improvement ↔ true productivity versus mix/settlement;
- capex decline ↔ utilization / maintenance needs;
- adjacent-business capital ↔ incremental return.

### Uncertainty source

A material portion is **future-state / structural uncertainty**, not simply missing current-PIT reading.

### Qualification result

```text
PROBABILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED
```

Reason:

> Assigning `15/25/35/20/5` today would produce more numerical completeness than epistemic information. The current evidence can define and challenge causal worlds, but does not support a defensible probability measure precise enough to make deterministic expected-return Odds decision-grade.

This is not the same as `Research incomplete`.

## 11. Research stop / resolution path

Current public-source research should stop here because the main discriminants are now future observations rather than easily discoverable missing facts.

Next high-value resolution nodes:

1. **Q3/Q4 unit economics** — unit revenue, transport/hub cost, express unit NP.
2. **Franchise/network evidence** — YTO-specific fee stability, network churn, service quality, regulator or endpoint stress signals.
3. **Rolling cash conversion** — rolling-12m OCF versus cash capex; utilization and asset efficiency.
4. **Competition discipline** — whether industry pricing remains rational when demand/peak-season conditions change.
5. **Adjacent-business returns** — aviation/international margin and capital absorption.

A future reopen should identify which variable actually changed.

```text
price only
→ update market-compatible world surface / Odds mechanics
→ Fundamental Belief unchanged

new operating / network / cash evidence
→ reopen Fundamental Research

new sell-side forecast only
→ update Published Expectations first
→ change Fundamental Belief only if it reveals admissible new evidence or superior causal reasoning
```

## 13. Architecture learning — not yet Constitution change

YTO provides one concrete case supporting the proposition:

```text
Research complete
        !=
Probability established
        !=
Odds ready
```

It also suggests a useful conceptual distinction:

```text
Research World
= causally coherent joint world

Odds Scenario
= Research World + probability measure + valuation translation
```

Current Kernel `Scenario` represents the latter, not the former.

One case is not enough to change Kernel Constitution. The next dogfood should test the same failure mode on materially different business models before opening a Constitution review.

A Constitution review becomes justified only if repeated cases show:

> Research is current-PIT complete and Human-useful, but the Kernel commit path systematically pressures Research producers to manufacture probabilities solely to obtain formal completeness / Odds eligibility.
