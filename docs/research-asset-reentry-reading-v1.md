# Research Asset Re-entry: same-reading association v1

Scope: #503 first thin reading delivery, under RM-20260922-r2 and Main Construction takeover. Reuse Decision: REUSE / THIN_ADAPTER. No new dependency or workflow.

The existing publisher, after its original reviewed-question and batch-disposition readers, builds `details/research/asset-reentry.json` and `.md`. The root `research.asset_reentry` is only an index to that same-reading detail. Original files, sources, results, failed runs, Human records and attention/launch state are not modified. The operation performs no remote GET, market request, model call, Research/Odds execution, notification or investment action.

## What is associated

The declared scope starts with existing exact-security purpose records, saved business/question work and registered on-demand archives. Empty NOT_STARTED business placeholders are not research assets. Radar/Stock and typed saved Watch can add context only to companies already possessing one of those records; they do not turn all Radar members into research tasks.

Available body references are checked with the existing same-package `retained_bytes` contract (path, length, Git blob, SHA256 and pinned reading rule). This proves byte identity, not source truth or present validity. On-demand archive links remain locators, not a claim that their bodies have been fetched. Missing bodies stay visible beside the asset rather than deleting its history.

Saved question IDs, execution IDs, versions, sources and original Human records remain separate. An exact ticker match is not a same-question or successor decision. A METHOD_SUPPLEMENT locator asks the reader to reconcile that particular earlier review; the association itself does not rewrite the thesis or declare every company version challenged.

## Routing boundaries

The result can direct recovery of missing bytes, reconciliation of existing method reviews, review of an already-saved price condition, comparison with an already-saved Radar/Stock observation, or recovery of a registered archive. No association in this reading is **not NO_CHANGE**. A quiet price condition is not proof all thesis evidence has been checked. Source failure does not invalidate a thesis.

The original Watch report is validated and bound to the exact retained Watch bytes. Its own observed time and statuses are preserved. This is historical saved attention context, not a fresh price qualification or new alert. The index never decides that a frozen Research distribution is numerical/eligible: any later price-only calculation must use the original Research/Market/Odds entry and independently establish those conditions. Probability-free/ordinal Research stays probability-free.

Human acceptance is read only through its original exact record and is never transferred to new Research or observations. Nothing in the detail is investment authority. Repeated identical input yields the same association; the projection emits zero new Research/attention events.

## Bounds and failures

At most64 companies,256 asset/locator entries and64 origins per matched Radar company; the existing32MiB saved-source bound and max512KiB generated report. These are bounded reading costs, not claims of complete historical coverage. Existing192KiB root-index, retained-output and publication-API reserves remain unchanged. No existing source reference is refetched merely for this view.

Optional question/Radar/Stock/Watch association failure rolls back that entire lane's additions and records a gap; independent valid assets remain. Publication is atomic: compose on a copy, check the original total-byte/API reserve, then replace the collector's file map. A budget failure cannot leave a half-published re-entry detail.

Synthetic markets remain explicitly synthetic. No local full-checkout or live market qualification is claimed.
