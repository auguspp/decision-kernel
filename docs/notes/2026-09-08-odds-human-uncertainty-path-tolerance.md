# Odds / Human Uncertainty / Path Tolerance — conversation note

Date: 2026-09-08

Status: **DOGFOOD NOTE / NOT YET A REQUIREMENT / NO SCHEMA CHANGE**

Authority: **NONE**. This note does not change Research, Odds, Human Attention, or Investment Authority.

## Trigger

A real-world observation prompted this note: some experienced investors can express an investment case in a highly compressed way — roughly, what they believe, what the market appears to expect, what may still be unpriced, what uncertainty they are willing to accept, and why the current price feels like acceptable odds.

The visible output may look like “盘感”, but it may be the compressed surface of a much richer internal process.

A related observation is that such investors may tolerate poor entry timing or large interim drawdowns (for example, being down materially before a later recovery), suggesting that **economic odds** and **path tolerance / participation style** are distinct dimensions.

## Working hypotheses to preserve

1. **Compressed judgment is not evidence of a simple process.**
   A mature investor’s short statement may compress business understanding, market-expectation reconstruction, variant perception, uncertainty, and price/odds reasoning.

2. **Research should not be required to eliminate every UNKNOWN.**
   Some decision-relevant uncertainty may remain unresolved. The later Human decision may need to express which unresolved uncertainties the Human is consciously willing to bear.

3. **“Unknown” and “willing to underwrite the unknown” are different states.**
   A possible future Human-owned concept is something like `accepted_uncertainties` / `underwritten_unknowns`, but this is **not** a Belief field, not a probability claim, and must not create AI Investment Authority.

4. **Economic Odds and Path Tolerance should remain conceptually separate.**
   Two investors can share the same Research and the same terminal economic worlds while making different participation decisions because they tolerate different interim paths, drawdowns, holding periods, or entry imprecision.

5. **Do not retrofit old Numerical Odds thresholds from one successful rebound.**
   A stock reversing before an old mechanical participation threshold does not prove that the threshold was wrong. Missing an eventual rally is an admissible outcome. Hindsight must not tune the system toward “catch every winner”.

6. **The newer Decision Hygiene direction already contains much of the relevant cognition.**
   Market expectations, expectation-gap work, Price-Implied Worlds / Implied Expectations Surface, ordinal or partial probability knowledge, and the possibility of withholding Numerical Odds are already the intended methodological direction. The immediate question is therefore not “invent a new Odds philosophy”, but whether real dogfood reveals an interface or decision-boundary gap.

7. **Potential future attention gap to test:**
   There may be cases where cardinal Numerical Odds are not epistemically qualified, yet a qualitative asymmetry between Fundamental Belief and Price-Implied Worlds is material enough to deserve Human judgment. If this repeats in real cases, it may justify a Human-attention reason distinct from `NUMERICAL_ODDS_QUALIFIED`. Such a reason would be an attention trigger only, **not** a Buy Signal.

8. **Do not confuse discrete Odds zones with the underlying continuous surface.**
   Existing Decision Rehearsal already preserves how far the current state is from the next participation zone. If real users perceive `INSUFFICIENT_ODDS` as too binary, first inspect Human-surface compression before changing the calculation or policy thresholds.

## Desired eventual Human-facing compression

A mature Decision Brief may ultimately need to compress the complex backend into a few questions:

- What do we currently believe?
- What does the market appear to believe?
- What may not be priced?
- Which important uncertainties remain unresolved?
- Which of those uncertainties is the Human willing to bear?
- At the current price, what is the relevant asymmetry?
- What would invalidate the case or force Research to reopen?

The goal is not to make the backend simple. The goal is to let a complex, auditable process produce a compact judgment surface without hiding uncertainty or pretending to have knowledge it does not have.

## Dogfood before schema

Do **not** promote the ideas above into production fields or new state machines yet.

Future PIT replays should preferably include several different failure/success shapes:

- a stock that reversed before the old Numerical Odds threshold and was therefore missed;
- a “bad news is fully priced” judgment that proved wrong and continued to fall;
- a case with a large interim drawdown where the thesis later proved broadly right;
- a case where the thesis itself was wrong.

For each case, inspect whether the newer Research / Price-Implied Worlds method already expresses the important judgment, and if not, identify the exact missing layer: Research, qualitative asymmetry, Human attention, participation policy, path tolerance, or Human decision capture.

Only concepts that repeatedly improve real decisions or failure attribution should graduate into schema.

## Non-goals

This note does **not** authorize:

- changing Odds thresholds;
- loosening Human wake gates;
- adding automatic Buy / Sell recommendations;
- treating price action as Fundamental Evidence;
- forcing cardinal probabilities where only ordinal knowledge is justified;
- adding position sizing or target-weight authority;
- implementing a new path-risk engine before dogfood.
