## 1. Why this case surfaced

The cold-start Attention dogfood identified a new policy / operating contradiction:

1. On 2026-01-20, the National Healthcare Security Administration created nationally standardized pricing items for robotic-assisted surgery, with navigation / participation / precision-execution tiers linked to the principal surgery price. Local authorities set actual fee levels.
2. Hunan subsequently implemented mechanical-arm fee ranges in May 2026, providing a concrete local example of the pricing framework moving from national category design to executable local tariffs.
3. On 2026-09-03, DRG/DIP 3.0 separately recognized robot-assisted procedures: DRG added nine robot-assisted groups across orthopedics, urology, gynecology and thoracic surgery; DIP added corresponding groups including knee, lumbar/pelvic fracture and femoral-neck-fracture categories.
4. Meanwhile Tinavi reported >28,000 procedures in 2026H1 and >180,000 cumulative procedures, but H1 revenue fell 16%, losses expanded and operating cash flow deteriorated.

The decision-relevant question is therefore not whether robot surgery is gaining clinical use. That is already substantially evidenced. The question is whether the payment mechanism can repair the broken bridge from **use → listed-company owner economics**.

## 3. What is established — standalone business

### 3.1 Clinical utilization is real

Company disclosures state:

```text
2025 procedures > 49,000
2025 year-end cumulative procedures > 150,000
2026H1 procedures > 28,000
2026-06-30 cumulative procedures > 180,000
```

Using the disclosed lower bounds, 2026H1 procedures increased at least ~27% versus the >22,000 H1 2025 comparison disclosed by the company.

This is meaningful evidence of real clinical use. It is stronger evidence than shipment ambition, installed capacity, or a TAM narrative.

### 3.2 Revenue conversion weakened in 2026H1

Reported 2026H1:

```text
revenue                         ~CNY105.27m / -16.0% YoY
parent net profit               ~-CNY70.11m
parent deducted net profit      ~-CNY97.29m
operating cash flow             ~-CNY133.61m
group gross margin              ~67.18%
R&D investment                  ~CNY78.37m / 74.45% of revenue
```

The company attributed equipment-sales weakness to intensified competition, slower whole-system order conversion, greater equipment pricing room and a lower direct-sales mix.

This matters because the company already has visible clinical use. The near-term bottleneck is no longer simply “will surgeons use it?”

### 3.3 Monetization mix is the key diagnostic

The H1 segment evidence points in two directions:

```text
robot-system revenue            ~CNY52.21m / materially down YoY
supporting equipment/consumables ~CNY33.02m / >10% YoY growth
technical services              ~CNY18.37m / down YoY
```

A rough diagnostic can be constructed, but it must not be mistaken for a reported KPI:

```text
supporting equipment + consumables + technical services
2026H1 ≈ CNY51.39m
2025H1 ≈ CNY50.56m

lower-bound procedures
2026H1 >28,000
2025H1 >22,000
```

Using those lower-bound procedure counts mechanically implies a rough revenue-per-procedure proxy falling by about 20%.

This is **not** decision-grade unit economics because:
- procedure counts are disclosed only as lower bounds;
- “supporting equipment” is not necessarily per-case recurring revenue;
- timing recognition can differ from procedure timing;
- service contracts may not map one-to-one to cases.

But it is an important challenger:

> **Clinical utilization growth has not yet produced proportional recurring-revenue growth.**

### 3.4 Standalone profitability requires a much larger economic base

At H1 gross margin of roughly 67%, sales + administration + R&D expenses were roughly CNY164m for six months. A simple annualized static-cost thought experiment implies approximately CNY0.49bn annual revenue would be required to absorb those three expense lines at the same gross margin, before allowing for financing costs, changing R&D intensity, working capital, or growth investment.

This is a challenger, not a forecast.

The conclusion is only:

```text
current standalone revenue base
<<
revenue / gross-profit base needed for operating self-funding
```

Therefore “more procedures” alone is insufficient. The quality of monetization and operating leverage are load-bearing.

## 4. Payment reform — why this is more than a headline, but still unproven economics

### 4.1 The policy chain is sequential

The 2026 policy sequence matters:

```text
Jan 2026
national robotic-assistance price-item framework
→
May 2026
first concrete local tariff implementation example in Hunan
→
Sep 2026
DRG/DIP 3.0 separately recognizes robot-assisted procedures
```

This addresses two different hospital frictions:

1. **Can the robotic-assistance service be charged in a standardized way?**
2. **Will the higher-cost robot-assisted case be recognized distinctly under bundled payment rather than averaged into a conventional disease group?**

The September change makes the second question more favorable in principle.

### 4.2 Hunan proves local implementation is possible

Hunan's May implementation provided differentiated fee ranges for mechanical-arm navigation, participation and precision execution. This demonstrates that the national framework can become executable local pricing rather than remain only a central guideline.

It does **not** prove nationwide economics or Tinavi-specific revenue impact.

### 4.3 A policy benefit can have mixed economics for Tinavi

The January national framework explicitly says dedicated consumables required for mechanical-arm use are included in the auxiliary-operation price composition rather than automatically charged separately.

This creates a two-sided economic effect:

```text
positive:
more explicit hospital reimbursement / charging framework
→ lower adoption friction / higher utilization potential

possible negative:
fee caps + bundled consumable treatment
→ pressure on disposable / per-case monetization
```

Therefore the correct question is not “policy positive?” but:

> **After local implementation, what happens to hospital contribution economics and Tinavi's realized revenue / gross profit per procedure?**

### 4.4 What would prove the policy transmission

Decision-useful evidence would be:

```text
province-specific DRG/DIP weights / tariffs
+ hospital case economics before vs after
+ robot procedure volume at affected hospitals
+ Tinavi installed-base utilization
+ equipment tender / ASP evidence
+ consumables and service revenue per procedure
+ gross margin / OCF conversion
```

Until then:

```text
PAYMENT FRICTION REDUCTION = PLAUSIBLE / PARTIALLY ESTABLISHED
TINAVI REVENUE IMPACT = NOT ESTABLISHED
TINAVI MARGIN IMPACT = NOT ESTABLISHED
TINAVI OWNER-CASH IMPACT = NOT ESTABLISHED
```
