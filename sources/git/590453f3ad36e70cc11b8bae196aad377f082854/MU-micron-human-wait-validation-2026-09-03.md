## 5. What would materially strengthen the thesis

The thesis would strengthen if, among other things:

- SCA coverage expands materially toward the stated target without weakening economic terms;
- protected revenue demonstrably carries a high trough margin while open-market exposure becomes a smaller share of owner economics;
- HBM and advanced-memory mix remains structurally high after near-term scarcity eases;
- FY2027 capex is large but produces visible incremental revenue / margin / cash economics;
- normalized FCF after depreciation / sustaining capital supports a meaningfully higher earnings floor than historical cycles;
- Micron demonstrates that strategic customers share more of capacity / financing / commitment risk without capturing all of the economics;
- the business can plausibly earn acceptable returns in a normalized supply environment, not only during shortage.

## 6. What would weaken or invalidate the thesis

Research should reopen more skeptically if, among other things:

- SCA growth stalls or terms become materially softer;
- protected revenue is much smaller than expected or mainly protects volume without protecting economics;
- customer renegotiation / enforcement risk becomes material;
- open-market DRAM / NAND normalization overwhelms the protected portion of the portfolio;
- HBM economics commoditize faster than expected;
- capex / depreciation rise faster than sustainable gross profit and owner cash;
- normalized EPS / FCF appears materially closer to the historical commodity-cycle framework than the strategic-memory rebase world;
- incremental ROIC fails to justify the scale of reinvestment.
